Start with the basics

  • Confirm your iPhone has a working internet connection.
  • Update SongDuel and iOS to the latest available versions.
  • Force-quit SongDuel, reopen it, and leave it active briefly so sync can finish.
  • If a specific song has no preview, use its Apple Music button. Apple Music does not supply a preview for every track.

Apple Music connection

  • Open iOS Settings and confirm you are signed in under Media & Purchases.
  • In the Music settings, turn on Sync Library.
  • Confirm SongDuel is allowed to access Media & Apple Music.
  • Open Profile in SongDuel, disconnect Apple Music, and reconnect it if authorization has expired.
  • Full-song playback and some library features require an active Apple Music subscription.

Disconnecting Apple Music does not delete or sign out your SongDuel account.

Library, ranking, and sync

SongDuel saves picks offline first, so a comparison should not wait for the network. Cloud backup and restore occur separately after you sign in.

  • SongDuel needs at least two eligible songs for a comparison. Top Songs and History unlock after three completed picks.
  • Your evolving Top 10 is an early estimate. Confidence milestones arrive for the Top 3 at 250 picks, Top 6 at 500, and Top 10 at 1,000.
  • Apple Music’s Most Listened Songs are displayed separately and do not determine your SongDuel ranking.
  • You can keep comparing while a large Apple Music library imports; SongDuel displays live progress as it runs.
  • If your daily progress is unexpected, check the Daily goal in Profile; it can be set from 1 to 100 comparisons.

Account and data controls

Open Profile in SongDuel to add or remove an on-device profile photo, replay the tutorial, disconnect Apple Music, clear app data without signing out, log out, or permanently delete your SongDuel account. Permanent deletion requires confirmation with the same Apple Account so SongDuel can revoke its Sign in with Apple grant before removing associated cloud data, subject to limited security, legal, and backup retention described in the Privacy Policy.

Contact support

Email ssmk.consulting@icloud.com and include:

  • a short description of what happened and what you expected;
  • the SongDuel app version;
  • your iPhone model and iOS version; and
  • steps that reliably reproduce the problem, if known.

Do not send secrets. Never include passwords, Apple credentials, authentication tokens, private relay addresses, or personal music history.
