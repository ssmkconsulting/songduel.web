Join your friends

Create a room on the host’s iPhone and share its six-digit code. Friends sign in and enter that code from their phones. A room supports 2–8 players. The selected player count must be present before the host starts. If a room has ended, ask the host to create a new one.

Music and snippets

Connect Apple Music from the app to browse your available library and playlists. If Apple requests account or privacy confirmation, complete it in the Music app and return to the game. Some recordings have no available preview; choose another version. Content depends on your account and region.

Same Room mode plays on the host’s phone. Remote mode plays on each participant’s phone. Check volume and your AirPlay output if you hear nothing. Use replay controls when available.

TV Stream

Use iOS Screen Mirroring to a compatible display and select TV Stream in the game. Keep the host connected. Players still use their phones to select songs and vote. If the display disconnects, follow the in-app connection help and use the host phone as a fallback.

Try the demo

Tap “Play demo · No sign-in needed”, choose players and rounds, then tap “Create demo room” and “Start the Battle”. The public demo uses original bundled music and simulated opponents. It works offline without an account, subscription, second device, or cloud room. Practice using real Apple previews needs an internet connection.

Account and photos

Profile includes Sign out, Disconnect Apple Music, and Delete account. Deletion requires confirmation with the same Apple Account used to sign in. Optional “Share photo with room” sends a small thumbnail to room participants. Turn it off if you prefer not to share a photo. See the game privacy policy.

Optional one-time tips

Open Support from the game menu or postgame controls. Select an amount, then tap Support to open Apple checkout. Tips are optional, not subscriptions, and do not unlock features or music. Availability and prices are provided by Apple. TestFlight uses sandbox purchases and does not collect real money.

If checkout is cancelled, you can keep playing. Pending purchases wait for Apple’s approval. For a confirmed purchase, Apple manages the receipt and any refund request through Report a Problem. Never send us payment-card information.

Contact support

Email ssmk.consulting@icloud.com with your app version, iPhone model, iOS version, game mode, and steps to reproduce the issue. Avoid including other players’ personal information.

Never send passwords, Apple credentials, authentication tokens, backend keys, or payment-card details. SongDuel Game is operated by SSMK Consulting LLC in South Carolina, United States.
