Privacy, in plain language.

Effective August 9, 2026. This policy explains how SongDuel handles information in the iOS app and on this website.

Scope

SongDuel is operated by SSMK Consulting LLC (“SongDuel,” “we,” or “us”). SongDuel helps you import music, compare songs, build personal rankings, review ranking history, and create a Top 10 playlist you request.

SongDuel does not sell personal information and does not use your information for cross-context behavioral advertising.

Information SongDuel handles

Account information

When you use Sign in with Apple, we receive an account identifier and, depending on your Apple choices, a name and email address or private relay address. Authentication records and sessions are managed with Supabase.

Music and ranking data

SongDuel processes music metadata from the library and playlists you choose to connect, including song, artist, album, playlist, artwork, provider identifiers, and availability details. It also stores your comparisons, skips, scores, rankings, ranking snapshots, and playlist publishing state.

Connected-service data

Apple Music authorization and user tokens are managed by Apple and MusicKit. SongDuel requests Apple Music access separately from account sign-in. Revoking music access does not delete or sign out your SongDuel account.

Website and technical data

This marketing site does not include advertising trackers or first-party analytics cookies. GitHub Pages and infrastructure providers may process standard request information, such as IP address, browser type, and timestamps, to deliver and protect the site.

How information is used

We use information to:

  • authenticate and secure your account;
  • import the library and playlists you request;
  • calculate rankings, movement, insights, and history;
  • create or refresh a SongDuel playlist only when you ask;
  • sync and restore data across your devices;
  • provide support, prevent abuse, and maintain reliability; and
  • comply with applicable legal obligations.

Service providers and disclosures

SongDuel uses Supabase for authentication, database hosting, and server functions; Apple services for Sign in with Apple and Apple Music; and GitHub Pages to host this website. These providers process information under their own terms and privacy commitments.

We may disclose information when required by law, to protect users or the service, in connection with a business transaction, or to vendors working on our behalf. We do not allow vendors to use SongDuel data for their own advertising.

Retention and security

Account and ranking data is retained while your account is active and as needed to provide the service. Deletion may be subject to short-lived backup retention or limited retention required for security, fraud prevention, or legal compliance.

SongDuel uses encrypted transport, platform security features, access controls, row-level database policies, and the iOS Keychain where appropriate. No security method can guarantee absolute protection.

Your choices and rights

From Profile in the app, you can disconnect Apple Music, delete local and cloud ranking data, log out, or permanently delete your SongDuel account. You can also manage Apple Music permission in iOS Settings.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, object to, or receive a copy of personal information. We may need to verify your request before acting.

Children

SongDuel is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy as SongDuel changes. The effective date at the top of this page will identify the latest version.

For privacy questions or requests, visit SongDuel Support. SongDuel is operated by SSMK Consulting LLC.
