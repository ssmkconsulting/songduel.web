Privacy, in plain language.

Effective September 3, 2026. This policy explains how SongDuel handles information in the iOS app and on this website.

Scope

SongDuel is operated by SSMK Consulting LLC (“SongDuel,” “we,” or “us”). SongDuel helps you import songs from Apple Music, compare them, build personal All Songs or playlist rankings, review ranking insights and history, and create a Top 10 playlist you request.

SongDuel does not sell personal information and does not use your information for cross-context behavioral advertising.

Information SongDuel handles

Account information

When you use Sign in with Apple, we receive an account identifier and, depending on your Apple choices, a name and email address or private relay address. Authentication records and sessions are managed with Supabase.

If you choose a profile photo, SongDuel stores the resized image in the app’s protected local storage on your device. It remains protected while the device is locked, is not included in your SongDuel cloud backup, and is removed when you remove it, clear app data, or delete your account from that device.

Music and ranking data

SongDuel processes Apple Music metadata from the library and playlists you connect, including songs, artists, albums, playlists, artwork, provider identifiers, and availability details. If you enable optional Automatic preference sync, SongDuel may also process supported favorites, recent-listening, Replay, recommendation, and related preference signals. It stores this music metadata and the preference results associated with your SongDuel account so it can sync and restore your experience.

Product interaction

SongDuel stores your comparison choices and ranking scope, ranking changes, skips, scores, ranking snapshots, manually selected favorites, sync status, and playlist publishing state to operate and personalize your rankings. Experience preferences such as automatic preview, preview length, Daily Goal, and reminder settings are stored on your device.

Connected-service data

Apple Music authorization and user tokens are managed by Apple and MusicKit. SongDuel requests Apple Music access separately from account sign-in. Revoking music access does not delete or sign out your SongDuel account.

Website and technical data

This marketing site does not include advertising trackers or first-party analytics cookies. GitHub Pages and infrastructure providers may process standard request information, such as IP address, browser type, and timestamps, to deliver and protect the site.

The website feedback form prepares an email on your device; it does not upload or store your feedback. Your email address is optional. If you do not share it, we cannot reply or ask for more detail. Information you send is then processed by your email provider and ours.

How information is used

We use information to:

  • authenticate and secure your account;
  • import the library and playlists you request;
  • calculate rankings, movement, insights, and history;
  • create or refresh a SongDuel playlist only when you ask;
  • sync and restore data across your devices;
  • provide support, prevent abuse, and maintain reliability;
  • review feedback and reply when you voluntarily provide a contact email; and
  • comply with applicable legal obligations.

Service providers and disclosures

SongDuel uses Supabase for authentication, database hosting, and server functions; Apple services for Sign in with Apple and Apple Music; and GitHub Pages to host this website. These providers process information under their own terms and privacy commitments.

We may disclose information when required by law, to protect users or the service, in connection with a business transaction, or to vendors working on our behalf. We do not allow vendors to use SongDuel data for their own advertising.

Retention and security

Account and ranking data is retained while your account is active and as needed to provide the service. You can delete local and cloud ranking data without signing out. Permanent account deletion asks you to confirm with the same Apple Account so SongDuel can revoke its Sign in with Apple grant before deleting your account and associated cloud data. Deletion may be subject to short-lived backup retention or limited retention required for security, fraud prevention, or legal compliance.

SongDuel uses encrypted transport, platform security features, access controls, row-level database policies, and the iOS Keychain where appropriate. No security method can guarantee absolute protection.

Your choices and rights

From Profile in the app, you can manage your local profile photo, replay the tutorial, enable or disable optional Automatic preference sync, disconnect Apple Music while keeping or deleting your personal ranking, delete local and cloud app data without signing out, log out, or permanently delete your SongDuel account. Automatic preference sync is off by default, and disabling it stops preference reads. You can also manage Apple Music permission in iOS Settings. Daily reminder notifications are optional and are requested only after you enable them.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, object to, or receive a copy of personal information. We may need to verify your request before acting.

Children

SongDuel is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy as SongDuel changes. The effective date at the top of this page will identify the latest version.
